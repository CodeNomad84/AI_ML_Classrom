# Sample data

> Provenance: new content. All files are **synthetic** but physically motivated; the Week 1
> flight logs are generated by `SyntheticFlightLogGenerator` in `src/aerospace_ai/utils.py`, the others by the `tools/make_weekNN_data.py` scripts below.

| File | Used in | Rows | Flight |
|------|---------|-----:|--------|
| `week01_uav_flight_log.csv` | Week 1 lecture, lab, slides | 14 940 | 30° turn (1.155 g), 1.8 g pull-up |
| `assignment00_uav_flight_log.csv` | Assignment 0 | 14 900 | 45° turn (1.414 g), 2.25 g pull-up |
| `assignment01_gps_track.csv` | Week 2, Assignment 1 | 300 | 5 Hz GPS, one 40 m circle at Mehrabad (origin 35.6892° N, 51.3134° E, 1190 m) |
| `assignment01_lidar_scans.csv` | Week 2, Assignment 1 | 50 | 10 Hz, 16-beam LiDAR in a 10 m × 6 m hall with a pillar |
| `week04_fuel_flow.csv` | Week 4, Practice 4 | 400 | Fuel flow of a small piston UAV vs airspeed, altitude, climb rate, mass |
| `week05_rover_log.csv` | Week 5 lecture, lab, slides | 2 533 | 16 rover drives towards a wall, 10 Hz, seed 5 |
| `assignment02_rover_log.csv` | Assignment 2 | 2 595 | 16 rover drives towards a wall, 10 Hz, seed 52 |

Columns: `time_s`, `accel_{x,y,z}_mps2` (specific force, body z-axis up, so +9.81 when
parked), `gyro_{x,y,z}_dps`, `baro_alt_m`, `airspeed_mps`, `temp_c`. Sampling: 50 Hz.

Deliberate defects (what students must find): one logger outage (rows missing), NaN dropouts
in barometer and airspeed, and single-sample spikes of about +40 m/s² in `accel_z`.

Why synthetic: the true answers are known, so notebooks and hidden tests can check them
exactly. To use a real log instead, export one with the same column names (for example from a
PX4 flight log) and point `DATA_PATH` at it.

Week 2 files (generated by `tools/make_week02_data.py`): GPS columns `timestamp_s`, `lat_deg`,
`lon_deg`, `alt_m`, `hdop`, with 8 fixes of HDOP > 5, 3 NaN fixes and 1 corrupted latitude
(+100°). LiDAR columns `timestamp_s`, `range_00_m` … `range_15_m` (beam i at −180° + 22.5° i),
with ~6 % dropouts (0.0 or inf), 5 dust echoes of 0.05 m and 4 fog-blinded scans.

Week 4 file (generated by `tools/make_week04_data.py` from the `fuel_flow_synthetic` loader in
`src/aerospace_ai/datasets.py`): `week04_fuel_flow.csv`, 400 rows, columns `airspeed_mps`
(16 to 36), `altitude_m` (0 to 3000), `climb_rate_mps` (−2 to 3), `mass_kg` (25 to 40) and the
target `fuel_flow_gph` (g/h, piston UAV power-required model with 3 % multiplicative noise,
idle floor 150 W shaft power). No defects: this table is for comparing models, not cleaning.

Week 5 files (generated by `tools/make_week05_data.py` from `RoverLogGenerator` in
`src/aerospace_ai/rover.py`): columns `run_id`, `time_s`, `lidar_range_m`, `wheel_speed_mps`,
`accel_x_mps2`, `gyro_z_dps`. Each run drives towards a wall and brakes; the LiDAR is valid from
0.3 to 12 m and returns 0.0 when no echo arrives. Injected defects, with exact counts
(lecture / A2): leading out-of-range zeros, MNAR (489 / 482); random dropout zeros, MCAR
(41 / 50); dust echoes (11 / 16); NaN bursts in wheel speed (19 / 25); accelerometer spikes up to
±25 m/s² (11 / 14); a frozen gyro in two runs, 31 identical samples each (runs 3 and 11 / 3 and
15); re-sent duplicate rows (25 / 32).

Regenerate (deterministic, seeded):

```bash
python tools/make_sample_data.py
python tools/make_week02_data.py
python tools/make_week04_data.py
python tools/make_week05_data.py
```
